Questions worth asking.

A few things teams ask before they switch. Have more? Book a call.

How is delight.ai different from a chatbot or a generic LLM?

A chatbot answers a single message. delight.ai builds a memory of the customer across every channel and conversation, then uses it to answer with full context — and to act on your systems on their behalf.

What does it integrate with?

Salesforce, Zendesk, Freshworks, Sprinklr, Intercom, Confluence, Google Drive, Notion, ServiceNow — out of the box. Plus a webhooks + REST API for anything else.

How long does it take to launch?

Most teams go live in 2–4 weeks, including connector setup, policy tuning, and a Watchtower QA baseline. We staff a launch architect on every deal.

Is it safe to run on regulated industries?

SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and regional data residency in KR · US · EU. PII redaction and policy guardrails are configurable per channel.
